HueControl

Privacy Policy

HueControl does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

What the app does

HueControl communicates only with Philips Hue bridges on your own local network in order to control your lights, rooms and scenes. It works without any online account.

Data we collect

None. No personal information, usage analytics, or telemetry is collected or sent to Aegis AO Soft LLC or any third party.

Data stored on your device

The addresses and access keys of the bridges you pair are saved only on your own computer so the app can reconnect. This information never leaves your device and can be removed at any time by deleting the paired bridges in the app.
