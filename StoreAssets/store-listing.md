## Description (full body)
Light Control for Hue is a fast, focused Windows app for controlling AND managing your Philips Hue
lighting from your PC. It talks to your Hue bridge on your local network, so it is quick
and works without signing in to any cloud service.

Control by room: pick a room and its lights appear below, ready individually or all
together, with synced room and light dimmers. Recall any scene with a click — each shown
with its own colour preview — and add or remove scenes right where you use them.

Manage everything from one Settings window:
• Devices — search for and add ANY Hue device (lights, plugs, motion sensors, dimmer
  switches, buttons), rename or remove, grouped by room and type.
• Accessories — see battery and last activity, and program a dimmer switch to control a
  room (its ON button recalls the room's first scene).
• Rooms & Zones — create, rename, delete, and choose which lights belong.
• Scenes — add built-in Hue scenes (Relax, Concentrate, Energize, Savanna sunset and
  more) to a room, or delete scenes.

Have more than one bridge? Group them into homes and switch instantly.
